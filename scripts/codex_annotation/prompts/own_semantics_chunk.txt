Do not read documentation, source, tests, Git state, labels, other runs,

worker transcripts. Read only the package and its listed sparse
`discovery_artifact` timelines. Write only {OUTPUT_FILE}.

Review candidates chronologically and maintain a pending-drag ledger. A card
missing from the bottom hand while elixir is unchanged is held, not played.
It is canceled if it returns to hand. It is released at the later transition
where the source card permanently leaves, its slot becomes a persistent
cooldown/replacement, and its arena unit/building persists or spell resolves.
A later spend can release a previously held card together with a newly dragged
card. First establish release from the later persistent elixir spend, hand
cycle, and arena continuation. Then back-propagate event_frame_index to the
first labeled deployment indicator belonging to that confirmed release. For a
troop/building this may be its card-name/level placement label or spawn effect
before the body becomes clear; for a spell it is the first physical projectile,
rolling object, or impact frame. Do not wait for a fully resolved body or later
hand refill. A floating label, translucent placement ghost, targeting ring, or
drag shadow never proves release by itself: if the card returns, elixir does
not persistently drop, or the hand does not cycle, reject the drag instead of
timestamping the preview. Emit multiple cards at the same frame only when their
first confirmed deployment indicators are actually the same frame.

For every candidate, transcribe displayed elixir immediately before/after,
the observed delta, total elixir cost of all cards released at that transition,
occupied slots before, and cooldown slots after. Elixir regenerates while a
drag is held: a true 1-cost release may show no net digit change, and a 2-cost
release may show only a 1-point drop. Accept this compensated spend only when
permanent hand cycling and an independent post-release arena effect both
confirm it. Set regeneration_compensated=true exactly when total released cost
exceeds the displayed delta; the difference must be at most one. Never infer
spend from a floating preview alone. In the final evidence object,
`elixir_drop=true` means a spend was confirmed, including a spend proven by
the one-point regeneration-compensation rule when the displayed digit did not
fall.

For a spell, a targeting circle, card label, reserved/temporarily empty slot,
or hidden elixir digit is still a held drag. Accept only after the targeting
overlay clears and a direct projectile, rolling object, or impact appears. If
the segment ends before that happens, reject it.
