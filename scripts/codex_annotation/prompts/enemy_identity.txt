Blindly identify only the already-discovered enemy onsets in {RUN_DIR}.

This must be independent of onset discovery. Do not read the annotation guide,
source code, tests, Git state, ground truth, human labels, other annotation
runs, replay caches, predictions, detector/tracker/OCR output, evaluation
reports, verification.json, localization.json, own sheets, or worker
transcripts. Read only:

- {RUN_DIR}/manifest.json (only `run_id`, `fps`, and `segment`)
- {RUN_DIR}/enemy_onsets.json
- the exact verification_artifacts and identity_artifacts cited by each onset

The onset file deliberately contains no card guesses. First classify side.
The bottom player is own/blue and the top player is enemy/red. A unit dragged
from the bottom hand, a floating bottom-hand card-name/cost label, a blue
health/level indicator, or a unit originating on the lower half and moving
upward is own and must be excluded. An enemy unit originates from the upper
side, has enemy/red indicators, or moves downward from an enemy deployment.
Arena position alone is not enough—track the body over time.

For retained enemy troops/buildings, identify the card only from the two
delayed, grid-free identity views. Track the same body across both views and
discount spawn glow, evolution/enchantment glow, jump/impact effects, shadows,
and overlapping units. If the views remain ambiguous, render an additional
opaque identity sheet while continuously tracking the same body, up to 6.0
seconds after onset. Prefer a later clear side/body view over an early
spawn/impact view. Do not put a guessed card name in its event scope or
filename.

For spells, identify the card from the cited onset/effect sequence, including
direction, projectile or rolling-object shape, and impact progression.

identity_artifacts to []. Cover every onset exactly once in `decisions`.
Do not modify any other file.
