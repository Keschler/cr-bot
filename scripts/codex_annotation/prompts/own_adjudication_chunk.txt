Independently adjudicate only the anonymous own-event proposals in
{PACKAGE_FILE} for {RUN_DIR}. The proposals are the union of two blind sweeps;
their source/model is intentionally hidden.

Do not read labels, evaluation reports, other runs, source/tests, semantic
outputs, or transcripts. Read only the package and every cited discovery/exact
artifact. Write only {OUTPUT_FILE}.

For each proposal decide exactly one accepted release or reject it. A true
release requires permanent card departure/replacement cooldown and a
persistent unit/building or resolved spell. Displayed elixir may regenerate
during a held drag: allow at most one point of compensation, recorded as
total_released_cost minus observed_elixir_delta. Reject previews,
emotes/overlays, ongoing combat, and a card that returns.

For spells, targeting overlay, label, reserved elixir, or a temporary empty
slot is not release. Require the overlay to clear plus a direct projectile,
rolling object, or impact. Fail closed when the segment ends first. Proposed
frames are hints, not truth.

For troops/buildings choose event_frame_index at the first labeled independent
arena deployment effect/body after release; for spells choose the first
projectile, rolling object, or impact. Do not timestamp the earlier empty hand
slot or targeting preview. In the evidence object, elixir_drop=true means a
confirmed spend, including an allowed compensated spend whose displayed digit
did not fall.
A floating card-name/level label, translucent placement ghost, targeting ring,
or drag shadow is still preview UI and never counts as the independent arena
body/effect. Confirmation must be a listed labeled frame 0.5–1.5 seconds after
event_frame_index.

If accepted, choose the closest supporting candidate and choose event and
confirmation frames only from its labeled discovery frames. Use filesystem
tools only to read the named package/listed artifacts and to write the one
required output JSON. Do not invoke renderers or inspect any other file; cite
its discovery artifact for both. Canonicalize The
